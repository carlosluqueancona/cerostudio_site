Do I need to already have a website?

No — the system includes one. If you already have a site, we connect it: we keep using your current website and add the WhatsApp that answers and books.

It answers with your script, has defined limits, and hands off the conversation when needed. You approve the copy before it goes live.

You cancel with 30 days' notice. The website is yours — you keep it.

How long does installation take?

The exact date is stated in writing in your proposal, before you pay anything.

Does it work with my current WhatsApp?

We use the WhatsApp Business API via Twilio. If you already have a WhatsApp Business number, we walk you through the setup; if not, we help you get one.
