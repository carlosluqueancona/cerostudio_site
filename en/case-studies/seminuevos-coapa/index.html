The client

An established dealership with a website stuck in the past.

Seminuevos Coapa is a certified pre-owned car dealership in Mexico City, with several locations, a documentation guarantee and monthly financing plans. The business was thriving; its digital presence wasn't.

Its catalog —dozens of cars that change every week— lived on an old website where publishing a new unit or removing a sold one was a slow process that depended on third parties.

  • Pre-owned cars
  • Certified units
  • Monthly financing
  • Multi-location
  • Mexico City

The challenge

A website from another era selling in this one.

The previous site dated back to 2013: a dense design, table-based listings, tiny thumbnails and even a visitor counter. On a phone —where most car buyers search today— it was practically unusable. And every new car depended on someone external to get published.

No responsive design

Built for desktop monitors from a decade ago. On mobile it looked cramped and broken —exactly where people search for their next car.

Impossible to update

Publishing a new car or removing a sold one required outside help. The real inventory and the one on the site never quite matched.

Invisible in search

Without a modern technical foundation, the site couldn't compete on Google —let alone in the new AI search engines where people already research which car to buy.

What we built

A modern catalog they run entirely on their own.

This was no cosmetic makeover: we rebuilt the site on a modern backend with a foundation designed to sell cars in 2026 —and to make the dealership the owner of its own content.

The process

Three stages. From the old site to the new one.

01 · Diagnosis

Understand the inventory

We mapped how the inventory rotates, how a pre-owned car buyer shops and what made keeping the previous site up to date impossible.

02 · Design

Redesign the experience

A modern, mobile-first design centered on finding and getting a quote on a car fast —a world away from the tables and thumbnails of the old site.

03 · Development

Build and launch

Catalog, search and a self-managed panel on a modern backend, with SEO and GEO built into the foundation. Delivered ready for the dealership to run.
