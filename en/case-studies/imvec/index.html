The client

A shop that machines precision, invisible outside its trade.

IMVEC is a precision metal machining shop with more than 20 years of craft. Machined parts, tight tolerances and clients who come back for the quality of the work. The product was never the problem —the problem was that nobody outside their circle knew they existed.

Their entire business ran on word of mouth: referrals from regular clients. No visual brand, no website, and no way for a new client to find them —or trust them— before the first phone call.

The shop

Two decades of craft. Now, with a brand.

Precision was never the problem —it was always in their hands. What was missing was a way for the world to see it. Today the logo we created hangs on the shop wall, next to the machines that built their reputation.

The challenge

No website. No logo. No presence.

In the intake form they said it plainly: "Everything comes through referrals from our regular clients." And what they expected from us, too: "for people to find trust and confidence in our service." That was the real brief —turning 20 years of reputation into something a new client could see and believe.

No visual brand

No logo, no identity. A business with two decades behind it showed up with nothing to set it apart or communicate its level of work.

Zero digital presence

No website, invisible on Google. A potential client had nowhere to find them, vet them or request a quote outside a phone call.

Hostage to word of mouth

Growth was chained to referrals. Without a channel of their own, every new client depended on someone else making the introduction.
