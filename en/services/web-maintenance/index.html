Frequently asked

What people ask the most.

How much does web maintenance cost in Mexico?

Basic plan from $800 MXN/mo (security + backups + monitoring + report). Complete plan from $3,500 MXN/mo (adds unlimited content changes + monthly optimization + priority support). For stores or complex sites, quote by volume.

What exactly is included?

Security updates, automatic daily backups, 24/7 uptime monitoring, content changes, monthly Core Web Vitals review, SSL renewal, vulnerability scanning and transparent monthly report.

What is NOT included?

Doesn't include new feature development, redesigns, massive content migration, recovery of hacked sites without previous backup, or hosting beyond the first year. If you need any of this, we quote it separately.

What platforms do you support?

Sites built by Cero Studio (on Cloudflare Pages), WordPress (with or without WooCommerce), Shopify, and common frameworks (Next.js, Astro). For exotic platforms we evaluate case by case.

Is there a contract? Can I cancel anytime?

Month-to-month, no annual contract. Cancel with 30 days notice. We deliver complete report, access credentials and recent backups. No lock-in, no penalties, no holding your work hostage.

What's your response time?

Site down: < 2h business hours, < 4h weekends. Content changes: within 24h business hours. Inquiries/improvements: < 48h. Priority plan cuts all in half.
