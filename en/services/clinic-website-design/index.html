05 — Frequently asked

What clinics ask the most.

How much does a clinic website cost in Mexico?

Starts at $499 USD (Cero Launch, ideal for independent dentists). Established clinics typically go for Cero Pro from $2,500 USD — includes blog, advanced SEO and scheduling. Multi-specialty centers in Cero Premium from $4,500 USD. See current promotions.

What must a clinic website have?

Clear information on services and specialties, location with map, contact details and hours, doctor profiles with license number, privacy policy per LFPDPPP, cookie policy, scheduling option, WhatsApp button and ethically appropriate testimonials.

How long does developing a clinic website take?

Between 2 and 4 weeks. First week discovery + design, weeks 2 and 3 development and content, last week testing, SEO and launch.

Can you integrate an online appointment system?

Yes. We integrate Calendly, Doctoralia, proprietary hospital systems or custom development with calendar, email/WhatsApp reminders and Google Calendar sync.

Does the site comply with privacy policy and medical data protection?

Yes. Privacy policy per LFPDPPP, cookie banner with explicit consent, HTTPS-encrypted forms and secure servers. For clinics with electronic medical records, separate storage compliant with NOM-024-SSA3.

Do you do local SEO so my clinic shows up in Google Maps?

Yes. We optimize Google Business Profile, configure LocalBusiness and MedicalClinic schema, structure content for local searches like "[specialty] near me" or "[specialty] in [city]" and provide guidelines for verified reviews.
