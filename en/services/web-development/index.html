06 — FAQ

What people ask the most.

How much does a professional website cost?

Three plans, all one-time payments. Cero Launch — a high-impact one-page site with 4 sections, live in 5 business days — $299 USD at portfolio pricing; regular price $499 USD. Cero Pro — 4 to 6 pages with blog, premium animations and advanced SEO — $999 USD; regular price $2,500 USD. Cero Premium — custom features, digital strategy and priority, direct follow-up with Carlos — from $1,800 USD; regular price from $4,500 USD. Portfolio pricing applies to 5 projects a month; once the spots are filled, the next project comes in at the regular price. See the plans. Selling online? That's a separate product: see Online Stores (Tiendanube plans from $6,000 MXN or a custom store, quoted by scope).

How long does it take to develop the site?

Between 5 and 21 business days depending on the plan. Cero Launch in 5 days, Cero Pro in 21 days. Premium or projects with online store are quoted by scope.

What technology do you use?

Modern stack: optimized HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Cloudflare Pages for hosting, headless or lightweight CMS depending on the case. Static sites when possible (faster, more secure). For selling online: Tiendanube — we are a certified Tiendanube Partner agency — or a custom store. No generic templates.

What do I need to send to get started?

Just the essentials: business brief (we provide a form), text content, high-resolution logo, color palette if you have one, and visual material. If you're missing something, we solve it in the discovery phase.

Where is the site hosted?

On Cloudflare Pages — high-performance free hosting with global CDN, SSL included and automatic deploys. No additional cost the first year. After that, Cloudflare Pages remains free in its standard tier for most sites.

What happens after launch?

We deliver credentials, training so you can edit your content, and a support period included for questions and minor adjustments. For ongoing support (updates, monitoring, backups), we have the web maintenance service.
