05 — FAQ

What people ask the most.

How much does a professional website cost in Mexico?

Starts at $499 USD (Cero Launch, 1–3 pages). For established businesses with a blog, premium animations and advanced SEO, Cero Pro from $2,500 USD. For online store or advanced features, Cero Premium from $4,500 USD. See launch promotions.

How long does it take to develop the site?

Between 5 and 21 business days depending on the plan. Cero Launch in 5 days, Cero Pro in 21 days. Premium or projects with online store are quoted by scope.

What technology do you use?

Modern stack: optimized HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Cloudflare Pages for hosting, headless or lightweight CMS depending on the case. Static sites when possible (faster, more secure). For stores: WooCommerce, Shopify or custom development. No generic templates.

What do I need to send to get started?

Just the essentials: business brief (we provide a form), text content, high-resolution logo, color palette if you have one, and visual material. If you're missing something, we solve it in the discovery phase.

Where is the site hosted?

On Cloudflare Pages — high-performance free hosting with global CDN, SSL included and automatic deploys. No additional cost the first year. After that, Cloudflare Pages remains free in its standard tier for most sites.

What happens after launch?

We deliver credentials, training so you can edit your content, and a support period included for questions and minor adjustments. For ongoing support (updates, monitoring, backups), we have the web maintenance service.
