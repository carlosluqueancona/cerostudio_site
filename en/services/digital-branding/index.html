Frequently asked

What people ask the most.

How much does a digital branding project cost in Mexico?

Surgical identity refresh for businesses with an existing site: from $1,200 USD. Includes logo, palette, typography, guidelines and application to your current assets. Comprehensive projects with complete system, social media assets and extended manual between $2,000 and $3,500 USD. If you're also doing a new site, identity is already included in any web plan — combined with web development it has special pricing.

How long does it take to develop visual identity?

Between 2 and 5 weeks. Discovery (1 week), conceptualization (1–2 weeks), design + iteration (1–2 weeks). For urgent projects with limited scope, we can compress to 10 business days.

How many revision rounds are included?

3 standard rounds on the initial concept. Most projects close in 2 rounds because we work in close collaboration from discovery. Additional rounds have extra cost only if the brief changes significantly.

Does it include trademark registration with IMPI?

We don't include the process, but we deliver files in required formats and connect with specialized firms. Registration has a separate cost of approximately $4,000 to $8,000 MXN depending on the class.

What file formats do you deliver?

Logo in SVG, transparent PNG, PDF and AI/Figma. Palette in HEX, RGB, CMYK and Pantone. Identity manual in interactive PDF. Social media templates editable in Canva or Figma as you prefer.

Is intellectual property 100% mine?

Yes. Once paid, all files and commercial usage rights are yours. Cero Studio only retains the right to include the project in our portfolio. If you need total confidentiality, we discuss it at the start.
