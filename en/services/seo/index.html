How do I get my site to appear in answers from ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini?

There are 3 technical layers:

1. Permissions. robots.txt and llms.txt declare which AI crawlers can index your content (OpenAI's GPTBot, Anthropic's ClaudeBot, Gemini's Google-Extended, PerplexityBot).

2. Structure. Schema markup (Organization, FAQPage, Article, Product), direct answers in question/answer format, quantified data, verifiable authors and citations to primary sources make the models cite you as a reliable reference.

3. Topical authority. Models favor sites with consistency, updated dates, demonstrable expertise (E-E-A-T) and mentions in other authoritative sites. We work all 3 layers and monitor citations monthly.

What is AEO or GEO and why does it matter now?

AEO = Answer Engine Optimization. GEO = Generative Engine Optimization. Both terms describe the practice of optimizing your site so AI-based answer engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) cite you as a source.

The difference with classic SEO: SEO seeks position in the SERP; AEO/GEO seeks to be cited in the generated answer. It matters now because a growing portion of search behavior no longer ends in a click — it ends in a chatbot answer. If you're not in their sources, you stop existing for that user.
