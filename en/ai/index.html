    03 — FAQ

    Just what you need to know.

    Is this ChatGPT?

    No. The answer is generated by Llama 3.3, an open AI model running on Cloudflare Workers AI with no internet access. It's not ChatGPT, Gemini or Google — but it answers the way they do when they have no clear data about your business: with whatever it learned in training. That makes it an honest sample of what happens when the AI doesn't know you.

    Why doesn't the AI mention my business?

    Almost always for three reasons: the AI can't confirm you exist as an entity (name, address and phone inconsistent across your site, Google and directories), it finds no content it can cite (pages that answer what you do, for whom and where) and your site doesn't speak its language (no JSON-LD schema, no llms.txt). None of that depends on being the biggest business in your city.
